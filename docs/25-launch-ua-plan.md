# 25 — Launch & User Acquisition

> **Zero UA budget.** The plan is organic, community-led, and built around the two features nobody else has.
> Phase 10.

---

## 1. Positioning

The store listing has one job: communicate the difference in five seconds.

> **Zero Hour — Build. Ally. Fight together.**
> Squad-shooter action, base building, and a world map — with **real voice chat** and **arena battles against your own alliance**. Skill matters here.

### The three differentiators, in priority order
1. **★ Alliance voice chat** — coordinate by talking, not typing
2. **★ Arena rooms** — fight your alliance mates risk-free; power-normalized so skill decides
3. **Fair by design** — nothing lost that can't be recovered; every paid path has a free twin

**What we do not claim:** better graphics, more content, or bigger scale than the established games in this genre. We would lose those comparisons. We compete on the axes where we're actually ahead.

## 3. Soft launch

| Stage | Markets | Users | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Closed alpha** | Friends, Discord | 20–50 | Does it work at all? |
| **Closed beta** | Philippines, Vietnam, Indonesia | 500–2,000 | D1/D7, crash rate, ★ feature adoption |
| **Open beta** | + Brazil, Turkey, Egypt, Mexico | 5,000+ | Economy tuning, ★ arena load, monetization |
| **Global** | Worldwide | — | Launch |

**Why these markets:** large mobile-gaming populations, low CPI, high tolerance for early-stage games, and genuine overlap with our target audience (`02 §4`). Testing in a low-cost market first means a bad first impression costs you very little.

### Gates between stages
| Gate | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Alpha → Beta | Crash-free > 99%, tutorial completion > 70% |
| Beta → Open | **D1 > 35%**, D7 > 15%, ★ voice adoption > 25%, ★ sparring > 35% |
| Open → Global | **D1 > 40%**, D7 > 20%, conversion > 1.5%, ARPDAU > $0.10, 10 concurrent 20v20 stable |

**If a gate fails, do not proceed. Fix, then retest.** A global launch on weak retention burns the one shot you get at organic discovery, and the store's ranking algorithm will remember.

## 4. Organic acquisition

### The Discord-first strategy
Counter-intuitive but correct: **build a Discord community even though voice chat is our in-game feature.**

Discord is where this genre's players already congregate to find alliances and discuss strategy. We meet them there for *recruitment and discussion*, then the in-game voice chat is what keeps their actual play sessions inside our app. We're not competing with Discord for conversation — we're competing for the moment-to-moment coordination that happens during a match.

| Channel | Tactic |
|---|---|
| **Discord** | Alliance recruitment, dev updates, bug reports, weekly ★ arena tournaments with in-game prizes |
| **Reddit** | r/AndroidGaming, r/IncrementalGames, r/gamedev devlogs. Genuine participation, not drops |
| **TikTok / Shorts** | ★ Arena clips — power-normalized upsets are inherently shareable ("HQ 9 beats HQ 24") |
| **YouTube** | Devlog series. The "solo dev builds voice chat for free" story is genuinely interesting |
| **App store optimisation** | Keyword-optimised localised listings, iterate monthly |
| **In-game virality** | Alliance recruitment links shareable outside the game |
| **Influencers** | Micro-influencers (5–50k) in target markets, paid in early access and in-game cosmetics, not cash |

### Why the ★ features are the marketing
- "Power-normalized arena where a free player beats a whale" is a **story**, and stories spread
- Arena upsets produce natural short-form video content, for free, from players
- Voice chat is a concrete, easily-explained differentiator in a genre where everyone else's listing sounds identical

**The devlog is a real asset.** Building alliance voice chat and real-time arena netcode solo, on free infrastructure, is a technically interesting story that reaches developers first and players second. Developers with an audience are cheap distribution.

## 5. Launch week

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| −7 | Store listing live, pre-registration open, Discord announcement |
| −3 | Server load test at 3× expected peak; verify backups and the restore drill |
| −1 | Feature flags reviewed; runbooks re-read; support inbox ready |
| **0** | Staged rollout 5%; monitor crash rate hourly |
| +1 | 20% if crash-free > 99.5%; first hotfix window |
| +2 | 50%; first live event; first ★ arena tournament |
| +3 | 100%; publish the launch devlog |
| +7 | Review D1/D7, first balance pass, post-launch retro |

**Have a hotfix ready to ship, not just ready to write.** The first 48 hours will surface something, and the ability to respond within hours rather than days is what separates a recoverable launch from a dead one.

## 6. Retention & community operations

| Cadence | Activity |
|---|---|
| Daily | Monitor alerts, answer support, check Discord |
| Weekly | Live event, ★ arena tournament, patch notes, one balance tweak |
| Bi-weekly | Community poll on what to build next |
| Monthly | New hero, new ★ arena map, devlog, ASO iteration |
| Quarterly | Season, major feature |

**Respond to reviews. All of them.** A developer who answers a 1-star review publicly and fixes the issue converts a critic into an advocate, and future readers see it. It costs nothing and it's the highest-leverage community work available to a solo developer.

| Metric | Minimum | Good |
|---|---|---|
| Installs | 50,000 | 250,000 |
| DAU | 2,000 | 10,000 |
| D1 / D7 / D30 | 35 / 15 / 8% | 45 / 25 / 12% |
| ★ Voice weekly usage | 25% | 45% |
| ★ Sparring weekly | 35% | 55% |
| Conversion | 1.5% | 3% |
| MRR | $3,000 | $30,000 |
| Store rating | 4.0 | 4.5 |

### Honest framing
Most games in this genre fail commercially. The realistic upside here is a **sustainable small business**, not a chart-topper — and that is a genuinely good outcome for a solo project on $0 infrastructure.

The two ★ features are what make it plausible: they give the game a reason to exist next to much better-funded competitors, and they give players a reason to tell someone else about it. If they land, we have a niche. If they don't, we'll know from the beta gates before spending a launch on it.
